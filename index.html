Privacy Policy

Steady Habit · Last updated: May 17, 2026

Short version: Your habit data stays on your phone. We don't sell or share your personal information. The only data we collect is anonymous app-usage and crash reports — both of which you can turn off in Settings.

1. Who we are

Steady Habit is an Android app published by Duckens Apps (sole proprietor: Duckens Michel). This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how you can control it. If you have questions, email steadyhabit.app@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

Everything you create in Steady Habit — your habits, daily check-ins, streaks, notes, and settings — is stored only on your phone in a local database. We do not upload this content to any server. If you uninstall the app, this data is permanently deleted unless you have exported a backup.

3. Data we collect

Steady Habit uses two Google services to keep the app stable and to understand which features are useful:

  • Firebase Analytics — records anonymous events such as "habit created", "paywall viewed", or "theme changed". No habit names, no check-in data, and no personal identifiers are sent.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — if the app crashes, an anonymous crash report is sent so we can fix the bug. Reports contain technical information (device model, Android version, the line of code that crashed). They do not contain your habit data.

You can turn both of these off at any time by opening Settings → Privacy and disabling "Share crash & usage data". Once disabled, the app stops sending any analytics or crash reports.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Pro features are sold through Google Play Billing. When you buy a subscription or the lifetime upgrade, Google handles the transaction entirely. Steady Habit never sees, stores, or processes your payment card. Google sends us a record that a purchase happened (which product, whether it's still active) so we can unlock Pro on your account. For details on how Google handles billing data, see Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Notifications

Steady Habit can send local reminders (e.g. "Don't forget your morning habit"). These are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device by Android's notification system. No reminder content is sent to any server.

6. Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or address.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, calendar, location, microphone, or camera.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share data with any advertising network or data broker.
  • We do not use third-party tracking SDKs beyond Firebase (Google's own service).

7. Children's privacy

Steady Habit is rated for general audiences but is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and you want their data removed, contact us at steadyhabit.app@gmail.com.

8. Your rights

You can:

  • Export your data — Settings → Export to spreadsheet (CSV) or Back up data (full backup file).
  • Delete your data — uninstall the app, or use Settings → Reset to wipe the local database.
  • Disable analytics and crash reporting — Settings → Privacy.
  • Cancel a subscription — through your Google Play account at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

If you live in the EU/UK, you have additional rights under GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, portability). If you live in California, you have rights under the CCPA. Email steadyhabit.app@gmail.com to exercise any of these rights and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Data retention

Habit data stays on your device until you delete it. Anonymous analytics and crash logs are retained by Google for a maximum of 14 months and then automatically deleted.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the "Last updated" date at the top will change and a notice will appear in the app on next launch. Material changes will require you to acknowledge the new policy before continuing to use the app.
